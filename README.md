## 📖 About

FreeCam Utility lets you detach your camera from your character and fly freely through walls to scout terrain, plan builds, and locate structures — all while standing still.

---

## ✨ Features
<a name="features"></a>

- **Camera Detach** — fly camera independently of your character.
- **Speed Control** — scroll-wheel adjustable camera speed.
- **Clip Through Walls** — camera passes through any block.
- **Coordinate Display** — camera XYZ shown in HUD.
- **Screenshot Mode** — auto-hides HUD for clean screenshots.
- **Toggle Key** — return to body instantly.

> 🔑 **100% free** — no key system, no linkvertise, no ads.
